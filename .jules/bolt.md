## 2024-03-05 - Audio Processing Optimization
**Learning:** Audio loops iterating over many items like resampling or mixing cause substantial overhead due to bounds checks, slow floating-point operations (like `floor` and `ceil`), and unoptimized channel loop orderings. Replacing them with Rust iterators, unswitching loops to avoid repetitive branching, and converting floating indices to integers using fast casting vastly improves DSP speed on large buffers (~2x faster), while remaining functionally identical.
**Action:** When optimizing DSP loops (especially in crates like `aether-audio`), prioritize eliding bounds checks using `iter_mut().zip()`, precalculate constants (like `1.0 / ratio` and pan levels) outside inner loops, extract channel branching `if channel == 1` out of the tight loops, and prefer direct indexing with integer casting (`as usize`) over math methods like `floor()`.
