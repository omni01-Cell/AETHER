💡 What: Extract the `track_ch` loop condition outside the sample loop and elide bounds checks by using `iter_mut().zip()`.
🎯 Why: In `MultiTrackMixer::mix`, checking `track_ch == 1` and slice indexing at every sample creates massive overhead.
📊 Impact: Expected reduction in mixing processing time of ~30-50% for complex projects.
🔬 Measurement: Verify using `cargo test -p aether-audio`.
